Current research

SilentEdge-LLM

Privacy-preserving sEMG silent speech recognition on edge devices

SilentEdge-LLM investigates robust silent-speech transcription from multi-channel facial sEMG. The system is designed around identity verification, a lightweight EMG encoder, an articulatory-aware representation, an EMG-to-LLM adapter and quantized Llama 3.2 inference on Jetson AGX Xavier.

  1. 1sEMG acquisition and preprocessing
  2. 2Identity verification gate
  3. 3Lightweight EMG representation
  4. 4EMG-to-LLM adaptation
  5. 5Local transcript generation
